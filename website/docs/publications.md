    <h2>
      From Neural Networks to Generative AI – A Hands-On Journey for Actuaries
    </h2>
    <p class="resource-meta">
      <em>Betty Zhu, Max Martinelli and Samuel Gifford</em>
    </p>
    <p>
      <a href="https://github.com/IFoA-GenAI-WP/2025-CAS-RPM-NN-GenAI-Workshop" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">
        Explore the workshop materials →
      </a>
    </p>
    <p>
      An on-demand Casualty Actuarial Society (CAS) workshop for actuaries and insurance professionals, delivered in consecutive years in 2025 and 2026 and returning in 2027.
      The session walks through foundational neural network models (Feedforward, CNNs, RNNs, Transformers) and culminates in hands-on experience with advanced GenAI techniques like Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).
    </p>
    <p>
      Participants build classification models and a mini GenAI app tailored for insurance, gaining the knowledge and tools to integrate AI into actuarial practice.
    </p>
    <p>
      Interested in the workshop? <a href="{{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}">Submit a question via Contact Us</a>.
